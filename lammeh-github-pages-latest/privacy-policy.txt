PRIVACY POLICY — لمّة · Lammeh
Last updated: 15 August 2026

THE SHORT VERSION
Lammeh does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are
no accounts, no tracking, and no ads. Everything happens on your own device.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
None. Lammeh is a self-contained app of question cards, roleplay scenes, and
party games. It does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location, photos,
or any other personal data, and it has no login or user accounts.

2. HOW YOUR DATA IS USED
Because we collect no data, we do not use, analyze, sell, or share any personal
data with anyone. Your language choice and how you play stay on your device.

3. THIRD PARTIES
The app is served as a static website (for example via GitHub Pages) and loads
fonts from Google Fonts so text displays correctly. These providers may record
standard technical request logs (such as an IP address) as part of delivering
the page, according to their own policies. Lammeh itself does not place
advertising or analytics trackers.

4. CHILDREN
Lammeh is designed to be family-friendly and contains no adult content. Since
no personal information is collected from anyone, none is collected from
children.

5. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY
If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted with a new date.

6. CONTACT
Questions about this policy? Contact us at: YOUR-EMAIL@example.com
